Ling Ren is an associate professor at the University of Illinois Urbana Champaign. 

He works on applied cryptography and distributed algorithms.

He wrote a [textbook on Consensus Algorithms](https://github.com/renling/ConsensusAlgorithms/) that aims to be beginne-friendly and to bridge theory and practice.
